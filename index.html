About

I’m a UI/UX designer who likes the unglamorous middle of a product: the review screens, the dashboards, the handoff notes. I start with the people doing the work, then shape the interface around what they told me.

On teams I’ve led research, kept design systems healthy, written developer tickets and run manual QA, so what ships matches what I designed. I work closely with CEOs, PMs, engineers and sales to balance user, business and technical needs.
